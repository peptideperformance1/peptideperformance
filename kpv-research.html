KPV
RESEARCH.

An educational overview of KPV, a short peptide fragment derived from alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone and studied primarily in laboratory and animal models involving inflammatory signaling, epithelial biology, and antimicrobial activity.

EXPLORE RESEARCH ↓

RESEARCH OVERVIEW

WHAT IS KPV?

01

PEPTIDE FRAGMENT

LYS-PRO-VAL

KPV is a three-amino-acid peptide sequence composed of lysine, proline, and valine and corresponds to the C-terminal region of alpha-MSH.

02

INFLAMMATION RESEARCH

IMMUNE SIGNALING

Experimental studies have investigated KPV in models involving inflammatory signaling, cytokine activity, and cellular responses associated with tissue irritation.

03

EVIDENCE STATUS

PRIMARILY PRECLINICAL

Most research remains laboratory or animal based. Human clinical evidence is limited and does not establish an approved therapeutic role.

MECHANISMS UNDER INVESTIGATION

INFLAMMATION, BARRIER & CELL SIGNALING.

Researchers have examined KPV in models involving inflammatory signaling and epithelial tissue biology, but these mechanisms remain experimental.

NF

NF-κB SIGNALING

Preclinical studies have explored possible effects on inflammatory transcription pathways, including NF-κB-related signaling.

BAR

EPITHELIAL BARRIER

KPV has been studied in experimental intestinal and epithelial models involving barrier integrity, inflammatory stress, and cellular transport.

MIC

ANTIMICROBIAL RESEARCH

Laboratory work has also examined antimicrobial properties of KPV and related melanocortin-derived peptide sequences.

CURRENT EVIDENCE

WHAT HAS BEEN STUDIED?

A

ANIMAL MODELS

INFLAMMATORY RESEARCH

Animal studies have investigated KPV-related effects in models of intestinal inflammation, tissue irritation, and inflammatory signaling.

C

CELLULAR RESEARCH

EPITHELIAL SYSTEMS

Cell and tissue models have examined uptake, barrier-related processes, inflammatory responses, and antimicrobial activity.

!

IMPORTANT CONTEXT

LIMITED HUMAN DATA

Existing preclinical findings do not establish clinical effectiveness, long-term safety, or a validated human-use framework.

RESEARCH LIMITATIONS

WHERE THE EVIDENCE IS STILL LIMITED.

KPV research remains largely preclinical, and findings from cellular or animal models cannot automatically be translated into clinical outcomes in humans.

Questions remain regarding pharmacokinetics, route-specific effects, long-term safety, clinical effectiveness, and the reproducibility of findings across human populations.

RESEARCH SOURCES

PUBLISHED REFERENCES.

Melanocortin-Derived Peptide Research
Published studies have examined the C-terminal KPV sequence of alpha-MSH in inflammatory and antimicrobial models.

Intestinal Inflammation Models
Experimental research has evaluated KPV in epithelial and colitis-related models involving inflammatory signaling and barrier function.

Antimicrobial Research
Laboratory work has investigated antimicrobial activity associated with KPV and related alpha-MSH-derived sequences.
