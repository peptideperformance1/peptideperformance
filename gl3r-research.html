GL3R
RESEARCH.

An educational overview of the investigational triple-receptor agonist studied for its effects on metabolic signaling, energy regulation, and body-weight regulation.

EXPLORE RESEARCH ↓

RESEARCH OVERVIEW

WHAT IS GL3R?

01

RECEPTOR ACTIVITY

TRIPLE-RECEPTOR SIGNALING

GL3R is being investigated as an agonist of three metabolic hormone receptor systems: GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon receptors. Researchers are studying how simultaneous activity across these pathways may influence metabolic regulation.

02

METABOLIC RESEARCH

ENERGY & WEIGHT REGULATION

Clinical research has examined changes in body weight, appetite-related signaling, glucose regulation, substrate utilization, and other metabolic outcomes associated with triple-receptor agonism.

03

CLINICAL DEVELOPMENT

HUMAN RESEARCH

GL3R has been evaluated in randomized clinical studies, including Phase 2 and Phase 3 research involving adults with obesity or overweight and related metabolic conditions.

MECHANISMS UNDER INVESTIGATION

THREE SIGNALS. ONE RESEARCH COMPOUND.

The scientific interest surrounding GL3R centers on the combination of three receptor pathways involved in nutrient sensing, appetite regulation, glucose metabolism, and energy balance.

GIP

GIP RECEPTOR

GIP signaling is involved in nutrient-responsive endocrine activity and insulin-related metabolic pathways.

GLP

GLP-1 RECEPTOR

GLP-1 receptor signaling has established roles in appetite, glucose-dependent insulin secretion, gastric function, and metabolic regulation.

GCG

GLUCAGON RECEPTOR

Glucagon receptor activity is being investigated for its potential contribution to hepatic metabolism, substrate utilization, and energy expenditure.

PUBLISHED EVIDENCE

WHAT HAS BEEN STUDIED?

P2

PHASE 2

OBESITY RESEARCH

A randomized Phase 2 trial involving 338 adults evaluated the compound over 48 weeks. Researchers reported dose-dependent reductions in body weight, with the highest-dose group showing a mean reduction of approximately 24% at week 48.

P3

PHASE 3

TRIUMPH PROGRAM

The clinical development program has progressed into Phase 3 studies examining obesity, overweight, metabolic disease, and associated health conditions across larger participant populations.

!

IMPORTANT CONTEXT

INVESTIGATIONAL STATUS

Despite advanced clinical research, GL3R remains an investigational compound. Ongoing research continues to evaluate long-term efficacy, safety, tolerability, and clinical outcomes.

RESEARCH LIMITATIONS

WHAT WE DON'T KNOW YET.

Strong early and late-stage clinical findings do not eliminate unanswered questions. Long-term safety, durability of outcomes, population-specific effects, and the complete risk-benefit profile require continued investigation.
