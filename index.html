Frequently Asked Questions

Important information before you proceed.

No. Products are offered for research use only and are not for human or veterinary consumption.

No. This static landing page does not process payments or store order information. Ordering takes place through EZFormz.

No. Ordering is restricted to adults age 18 or older who can legally purchase and use research materials in their jurisdiction.

Does Peptide Performance provide medical advice?

No. Nothing on this site or the order page is medical advice, diagnosis, treatment guidance, or a recommendation for personal use.
