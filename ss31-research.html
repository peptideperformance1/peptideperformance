SS-31
RESEARCH.

An educational overview of SS-31, a mitochondria-targeted tetrapeptide investigated for its interactions with mitochondrial membranes, cardiolipin, cellular energetics, and oxidative stress.

EXPLORE RESEARCH ↓

RESEARCH OVERVIEW

WHAT IS SS-31?

01

MITOCHONDRIAL RESEARCH

TARGETED TETRAPEPTIDE

SS-31 is a synthetic four-amino-acid peptide designed to concentrate at the inner mitochondrial membrane.

02

MEMBRANE BIOLOGY

CARDIOLIPIN

A major area of investigation involves interactions between SS-31 and cardiolipin, a phospholipid important to mitochondrial membrane structure and function.

03

CLINICAL DEVELOPMENT

HUMAN RESEARCH

SS-31, also studied clinically as elamipretide, has progressed beyond laboratory research into human clinical studies involving several mitochondrial-related conditions.

MECHANISMS UNDER INVESTIGATION

MITOCHONDRIAL FUNCTION.

Research has focused on how SS-31 may influence mitochondrial membrane function and cellular energy production.

CL

CARDIOLIPIN INTERACTION

Experimental evidence suggests SS-31 interacts with cardiolipin within the inner mitochondrial membrane, an area important to respiratory-chain organization.

ATP

CELLULAR ENERGY

Researchers have investigated whether changes in mitochondrial membrane function can influence oxidative phosphorylation and cellular energy production.

ROS

OXIDATIVE STRESS

Preclinical studies have examined SS-31 in models involving mitochondrial reactive oxygen species and oxidative stress.

RESEARCH AREAS

WHAT HAS BEEN STUDIED?

M

MITOCHONDRIAL BIOLOGY

BIOENERGETICS

Laboratory studies have investigated mitochondrial respiration, membrane integrity, ATP production, and cellular responses to mitochondrial stress.

T

TISSUE RESEARCH

MULTIPLE SYSTEMS

Preclinical research has included skeletal muscle, cardiac tissue, kidney, nervous-system, and other models affected by mitochondrial dysfunction or oxidative stress.

H

HUMAN STUDIES

CLINICAL INVESTIGATION

Elamipretide has been evaluated in clinical research involving mitochondrial disease and other conditions in which impaired mitochondrial function may contribute to disease biology.

IMPORTANT CONTEXT

RESEARCH DOES NOT EQUAL PROVEN BENEFIT.

Mechanistic findings and promising results from experimental models do not by themselves demonstrate clinical effectiveness.

Results can differ substantially between cellular studies, animal models, individual clinical trials, diseases, endpoints, and study populations.

RESEARCH LIMITATIONS

WHAT WE DON'T KNOW YET.

Although SS-31 has a larger body of research than many experimental peptides, clinical results must be interpreted according to the specific disease, trial design, endpoints, and population studied.

Findings from one experimental model or clinical condition cannot automatically be generalized to unrelated uses, healthy populations, athletic performance, or other outcomes.

RESEARCH SOURCES

PUBLISHED REFERENCES.

Mitochondrial Targeting Research
Foundational SS-31 research has examined selective mitochondrial localization and interactions with the inner mitochondrial membrane.

Cardiolipin Research
Experimental studies have investigated interactions between SS-31 and cardiolipin and their potential influence on mitochondrial membrane and respiratory function.

Oxidative Stress Research
Cellular and animal models have evaluated SS-31 in conditions involving mitochondrial oxidative stress and impaired bioenergetics.

Clinical Research
Elamipretide has undergone human clinical investigation across several mitochondrial and mitochondrial-associated disease settings.
