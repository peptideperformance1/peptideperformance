Peptide Performance Frequently Asked Questions.

Straightforward answers about Peptide Performance, research-use requirements, ordering, shipping, educational information, and support.

What is Peptide Performance?
Peptide Performance is a research-focused platform providing access to research materials along with educational resources covering peptide and performance-related research topics.
Are your products for human use?
No. Products referenced or offered through Peptide Performance are intended for research use only and are not intended for human or veterinary consumption.
Do you provide medical or dosing advice?
No. Peptide Performance does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, dosing protocols, or personal-use instructions.
Where can I learn about individual research materials?
Visit the Research section of the website. Individual research pages summarize areas being investigated and provide links to published scientific literature where available.
How do I place an order?
Select Order from the website navigation and choose the appropriate ordering option. Current availability and applicable ordering information are shown through the order form.
Who may order?
Ordering is limited to adults age 18 or older who understand that materials are offered strictly for research purposes.
How long does shipping take?
Shipping time depends on the selected ordering option, destination, product availability, and carrier conditions. Any applicable shipping information is provided during the ordering process.
Do you guarantee research results?
No. Research findings vary by compound, study design, model, methodology, and evidence quality. Information provided on this website is educational and should not be interpreted as a guarantee of any particular research outcome.
Where does the research information come from?
Educational research pages reference publicly available scientific literature and published research. Evidence may include laboratory, animal, preclinical, and human studies with varying levels of quality and certainty.
How can I contact Peptide Performance?
Contact information and support options are provided through the website for questions involving the site, ordering, shipping, or general support.
Research Use Only: Information provided by Peptide Performance is for educational and research purposes only. It is not medical advice and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
