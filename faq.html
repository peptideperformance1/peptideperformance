PEPTIDE PERFORMANCE FAQ

GOOD QUESTIONS. CLEAR ANSWERS.

What is Peptide Performance?
Peptide Performance is a research-focused website that brings together educational peptide information and links to research-material ordering options. Our goal is to make research topics and ordering categories easier to navigate.
Are these materials intended for human use?
No. Research materials referenced through Peptide Performance are described for research use only, not human or veterinary consumption. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease. Research-use labeling does not itself establish regulatory approval or legal sale.
Do you provide dosing or medical advice?
No. We do not provide dosing protocols, personal-use instructions, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations. Educational research summaries are not a substitute for professional medical advice.
Where can I learn about specific peptides?
Start with the Peptide Research Library, where you can browse 12 research topics and open individual pages about the available evidence and its limitations.
What is the difference between US Warehouse and Overseas Research Orders?
These are separate ordering channels. US Warehouse Singles and Overseas Research Orders may differ in item selection, package format, fulfillment location, shipping process, and availability. Review the current details for each option on our ordering information page before submitting a request.
How do I place an order?
Go to Ordering Information and select the ordering channel that fits your research requirements. Follow the instructions on the external form and review its current availability, terms, and requirements.
Who is eligible to order?
Ordering is intended for eligible adults conducting legitimate research, subject to the applicable laws, product restrictions, and requirements of the relevant ordering service. Being 18 or older does not by itself establish eligibility.
How long does shipping take?
Timing varies with the ordering channel, destination, inventory, carrier, and any customs or processing requirements. We do not guarantee a fixed delivery window on this page. Consult the current ordering form or fulfillment contact for estimates and updates.
Will I receive tracking information?
Tracking availability depends on the selected fulfillment channel and shipping method. Refer to the information provided through your order confirmation or the relevant ordering service.
Are research outcomes or product effects guaranteed?
No. Published findings depend on study methods, compound quality, research models, and many other factors. Laboratory or animal findings should not be treated as proof of clinical benefit or safety in people.
Where does the research information come from?
Our educational pages summarize topics discussed in publicly available scientific literature. Studies vary in quality and may involve cell cultures, animals, or limited human data. We aim to describe those differences rather than present early findings as established facts.
How can I get help with an order?
For a submitted order, use the contact or support instructions supplied by the ordering service or in your confirmation. For general website information, review our About page and the links on the ordering page.
